BlockWorm data deletion

These instructions apply to Blocks & Worms: Block Puzzle on Android. The game appears as BlockWorm in Facebook and may also appear under that name in Android Settings.

Delete data in the game

Open Settings > Delete Data (shown as Delete my account in some versions), read the confirmation, and confirm only if you want to reset your data. Keep the internet connection available. The game first deletes the associated Google Play Games cloud save and currently connected Facebook social data, then revokes Facebook access and clears local progress. If a connected service fails, the game keeps the deletion pending so you can retry; do not assume deletion succeeded until it completes.

Social deletion removes your profile, shared progress, associated friend gift records, club membership and requests. An ordinary club transfers leadership to another member or is dissolved if empty; the system-owned official club remains. Club gifts already sent to other members may remain without your profile ID. A hashed deletion marker and timestamp are retained to prevent old requests from rebuilding deleted records. If you used more than one Facebook account, reconnect each account whose social data you want deleted, or contact Support for help.

This does not delete your Facebook or Google account, cancel purchases, or erase Google Play order history. To remove remaining local copies, clear game data on your other devices before continuing play.

Disconnect Facebook

  1. In the game, open Settings and select Sign out of Facebook to clear the active Facebook login session on this device.
  2. In Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites. Select BlockWorm, choose Remove, and confirm. You can do this even if you have already uninstalled the game.

Disconnecting Facebook leaves your local game progress and existing server-side social records available. Use Delete Data or contact Support if you also want those records removed.

Delete a Google Play Games cloud save

Close the game on your devices. In your Google Play Games profile, open Your Data, choose the option to delete game data, and under individual games select BlockWorm. Google provides instructions for web and Android. To remove all copies, also clear the local game data on each device before reopening the game. If you keep playing while connected, the game can create a new cloud save.

Erase your local game data

On each Android device where you played, open Settings → Apps → BlockWorm → Storage → Clear storage (sometimes called Clear data). This permanently clears the game’s local save, including levels, wallet, items, stored purchase fulfilment records, preferences and cached login data on that device. Google Play manages your order history separately.

Request deletion from trifun

If you contacted support or need help removing app-related data, email crazyrabbit@gmail.com with the subject “BlockWorm data deletion”. For support correspondence, send the request from the address you used for support. For social data, identify the game and the Facebook profile used to connect; trifun may ask for minimal information to verify ownership before removing records. Do not send passwords, login codes or payment details. trifun will use the information to locate and handle your request and respond by email.
