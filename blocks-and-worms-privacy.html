Blocks & Worms Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Android game Blocks & Worms: Block Puzzle and messages you choose to send to trifun about it.

While you play

The game stores your level progress, current puzzle, in-game currency and items, and settings on your device so you can continue playing. You can play without creating an account. The game does not send this information to trifun or to advertising or analytics services.

Purchases through Google Play

If you buy an item, Google Play processes the payment. The game receives the product ID, purchase status and signed purchase details from Google Play. It verifies the receipt on your device and stores a digest of the purchase token with your local wallet to prevent duplicate delivery. The game sends the purchase token back to Google Play to confirm delivery or mark an item as consumed. trifun does not receive your card or bank details. Google manages its own purchase history under the Google Privacy Policy.

Your local save

Your game data stays in the app’s local storage until you clear it. To remove it, open Android Settings, select Blocks & Worms under Apps, then select Storage and clear the app’s data. This resets your progress. Android backup or restore, if enabled on your device, is managed through your device settings.

If you email us

We receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and follow up on your request. Our email provider processes the correspondence to deliver and store it. We retain support messages for as long as needed to handle the request and any necessary follow-up. You may ask us to delete your correspondence by writing to the address below. We do not sell your information.
