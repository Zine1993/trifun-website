Blocks & Worms Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Android game Blocks & Worms: Block Puzzle and messages you choose to send to trifun about it.

While you play

The game stores your level progress, current puzzle, in-game currency and items, and settings on your device so you can continue playing. You can play without creating an account. The game does not send this information to trifun or to advertising or analytics services.

Google Play Games and cloud saves

In versions that support Google Play Games, Google checks your Play Games sign-in when you open the game. If you are connected, the game uses your game-specific player ID to associate this device’s save with your profile. It sends completed levels, coins, item counts, lives, reward claims and purchase-token digests to Google Play Games Saved Games so you can continue on another Android device using the same Play Games profile. Google stores this in the app’s private saved-game space. If two devices have different progress, the game may ask which save to keep. Offline changes are kept on your device until synchronization succeeds.

See the Google Privacy Policy and our data deletion instructions.

Optional Facebook login

In versions with Facebook login, you can choose Sign in with Facebook in Settings. Facebook authenticates you and provides an app-specific user ID and a login session through its Android SDK. The game requests the public_profile permission and uses the SDK’s session to show whether you are signed in. The SDK keeps the session on your device and communicates with Meta to support login. trifun does not copy the access token into your game save or maintain a server-side Facebook player profile. Facebook login does not synchronize your progress or wallet. Meta describes its processing in the Meta Privacy Policy.

To disconnect Facebook, erase local game data, or ask trifun to delete support correspondence, follow the BlockWorm data deletion instructions.

Purchases through Google Play

If you buy an item, Google Play processes the payment. The game receives the product ID, purchase status and signed purchase details from Google Play. It verifies the receipt on your device and stores a digest of the purchase token with your local wallet to prevent duplicate delivery. The game sends the purchase token back to Google Play to confirm delivery or mark an item as consumed. trifun does not receive your card or bank details. Google manages its own purchase history under the Google Privacy Policy.

Billing diagnostics

When the game connects to Google Play, the billing library sends Google technical diagnostics, such as the billing library version and billing results or errors. Google uses this information to monitor and improve billing reliability. The game does not provide a separate switch for these diagnostics.

Your local save

Your game data stays in the app’s local storage until you clear it. To remove it, open Android Settings, select Blocks & Worms under Apps, then select Storage and clear the app’s data. This resets your progress. Android backup or restore, if enabled on your device, is managed through your device settings.

If you email us

We receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and follow up on your request. Our email provider processes the correspondence to deliver and store it. We retain support messages for as long as needed to handle the request and any necessary follow-up. You may ask us to delete your correspondence by writing to the address below. We do not sell your information.
