About this notice

This notice covers the Trifun studio website at trifun.bot.cd, operated by trifun. PetCrossStitch and Narraia are separate services; their data practices are not described by this notice.

Browsing this website

This is a static information website. It has no registration, photo uploads, payments, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts. The language switch works within your current visit and does not use cookies or local storage.

Hosting and external links

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to serve and secure the website. When you follow a product link, the destination website’s own notices and practices apply.

Email contact

If you email crazyrabbit@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use that information to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other information that is unnecessary for your question. You can use the same address to ask about your correspondence or request its deletion.
