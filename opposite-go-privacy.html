Opposite Go Privacy Policy

Updated: October 12, 2026 · Android package: com.oppositego.game

Opposite Go is published and operated by trifun. Contact crazyrabbitnow@gmail.com for privacy questions.

Which version this covers

Version 0.2.9 and earlier do not include analytics. Starting with version 0.2.10, the game offers optional gameplay analytics as described below. The game remains playable offline, without an account, advertising, in-app purchases or cloud saves.

Optional gameplay analytics

Analytics is off until you choose “Allow analytics”. If you allow it, Google Analytics for Firebase processes an app-instance identifier, device and app information (such as model, operating system, language and app version), approximate location derived from network IP information, session activity, and gameplay events. Events include starting and completing the tutorial, starting and ending a run, score, rounds played, elapsed time, combo, failures and their causes, replaying, and leaving a run.

We use these data only to understand usage and retention and improve the game's tutorial and difficulty. We do not send your name, email, account ID, precise location, contacts, photos or payment details. Advertising ID collection and advertising personalization are disabled. We do not sell these data, link Google Ads, or enable Google Signals for this version. Analytics uses an app-instance identifier rather than your name; this is pseudonymous data, not a guarantee of anonymity.

Your choice

You can choose “No analytics” and use the entire game. Open Settings → Analytics at any time to change your choice. Turning analytics off stops new collection and resets the analytics identifier and cached analytics data on your device. It does not erase data already received by Google. Data from players who opt out is not included in our gameplay analytics.

Local game data

Settings, consent choice, tutorial progress, selected characters, coins and gameplay records are stored on your device. Your save file is not uploaded or backed up by us. If analytics is enabled, selected gameplay measurements described above are sent separately. Optional vibration provides gameplay feedback.

Service provider, security and retention

Google processes analytics for trifun through Google Analytics for Firebase. Analytics is encrypted in transit using TLS and may be processed on Google's servers outside your country. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Analytics data disclosures. Event and user data are retained according to our Google Analytics retention settings; aggregated reporting may be retained longer. Access is restricted to authorized people administering the game.

Deletion and support

Clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it to delete local progress and preferences. This does not delete analytics already sent. There is no online game account to delete. For privacy or deletion inquiries, contact the email above. Because analytics is not linked to your email or a game account, we may be unable to identify your individual analytics records from an email address alone. Disabling analytics prevents future collection; it is not a remote-deletion request.

If you email support, we receive your email address and message and use them to answer your request. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve the request or meet applicable requirements. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

Other services and age audience

Google Play and your operating system may independently process store and app-quality information under their own policies. Opening this page discloses normal request information, such as an IP address, to the website host. Opposite Go is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13.

Updates

We will update this policy before introducing changes to these data practices.

Opposite Go 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 12 日 · Android 包名：com.oppositego.game

Opposite Go 由 trifun 发布和运营。隐私问题请联系 crazyrabbitnow@gmail.com。

适用版本

0.2.9 及更早版本不包含统计分析。自 0.2.10 起，游戏提供下述可选玩法统计。游戏仍可离线游玩，无需账号，不包含广告、应用内购买或云存档。

可选玩法统计

在你选择“允许统计”之前，统计功能保持关闭。同意后，Google Analytics for Firebase 会处理应用实例标识、设备和应用信息（例如型号、操作系统、语言和版本）、根据网络 IP 信息推导的大致地区、会话活动及玩法事件。事件包括教程开始与完成、单局开始与结束、分数、回合数、时长、连击、失败原因、重玩及离开对局。

这些数据仅用于了解使用情况和留存，改进教程与难度。我们不会发送姓名、邮箱、账号 ID、精确位置、通讯录、照片或支付资料。广告 ID 采集及广告个性化已关闭。本版本不出售这些数据，不关联 Google Ads，也不启用 Google Signals。统计使用应用实例标识，而非姓名；这属于假名化数据，并不意味着绝对匿名。

你的选择

选择“不允许统计”也可以完整游玩。你可随时进入“设置 → 玩法统计”修改选择。关闭后停止新增采集，并重置设备上的统计标识和缓存统计数据；这不会删除 Google 已接收的数据。选择关闭的玩家不计入我们的玩法统计。

本地游戏数据

设置、统计选择、教程进度、角色、金币和游戏记录保存在设备上。我们不上传或备份存档文件。开启统计时，上述部分玩法指标会单独上传。可选振动仅用于玩法反馈。

服务商、安全与保留

Google 通过 Google Analytics for Firebase 为 trifun 处理统计数据。传输使用 TLS 加密，数据可能在你所在国家以外的 Google 服务器上处理。参见 Google 隐私政策及 Firebase Analytics 数据披露。用户和事件数据按 Google Analytics 的保留设置存储，汇总报告可能保留更久。访问仅限获授权的游戏管理人员。

删除与客服

在 Android 设置中清除应用存储或卸载游戏，可删除本地进度和偏好，但不会删除已发送的统计数据。游戏没有需要注销的在线账号。隐私或删除咨询请联系上述邮箱。统计未关联邮箱或游戏账号，因此仅凭邮箱地址可能无法定位你的单独统计记录。关闭统计可阻止后续采集，但不等于申请删除远端数据。

你主动发邮件时，我们会收到邮箱和邮件内容，仅用于答复请求，并按解决请求或适用要求所需保留。请勿发送不必要的敏感资料。

其他服务与年龄

Google Play 和操作系统可能按各自政策独立处理商店及应用质量信息。打开本网页时，托管服务会处理 IP 地址等常规请求信息。游戏面向 13 岁及以上玩家，不专门面向 13 岁以下儿童。

政策更新

数据处理方式发生变化时，我们会在引入相关变化前更新本政策。
