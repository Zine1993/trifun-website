Opposite Go Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 · Android package: com.oppositego.game

This policy describes the initial Android release of Opposite Go, published and operated by trifun. For privacy questions, contact crazyrabbitnow@gmail.com.

Data in the game

Opposite Go works offline and does not require an account. The game does not collect or transmit personal data to us or third parties. It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, cloud saves, or in-app purchases, and does not access the Android advertising ID.

The game stores settings, tutorial progress, selected characters, in-game progress and coins, scores, and gameplay records locally on your device. These records support gameplay and remember your preferences. They are not uploaded to us. Vibration is used only for optional gameplay feedback.

Storage and deletion

Local game data remains in the app's storage until you clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. Clearing storage permanently resets your local progress and preferences. There is no online account or server-side game profile to delete.

Support messages

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the content you send. We use that information to answer and resolve your request, restrict access to those handling support, and retain it only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal requirements. You may request deletion by contacting the same address. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information. Sending an email is separate from playing the game.

Sharing and external services

We do not sell game data or share it with advertisers. Google Play, your operating system, your email provider, and websites you choose to open operate under their own privacy policies. Visiting this policy page may disclose normal technical request information, such as an IP address, to its website hosting provider; this is separate from the offline game.

Age audience

Opposite Go is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The game does not ask for age or other personal details.

Changes

We will update this policy and its effective date if the game's data practices change. This policy describes the current release; future features will be reflected here before they are introduced.

Opposite Go 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 8 日 · Android 包名：com.oppositego.game

本政策适用于 trifun 发布和运营的 Opposite Go Android 首发版本。隐私问题请联系 crazyrabbitnow@gmail.com。

游戏中的数据

本游戏可离线运行，无需账号，不向我们或第三方收集、上传个人数据。游戏不包含广告、统计分析、追踪、云存档或应用内购买，也不访问 Android 广告 ID。

设置、教程进度、所选角色、游戏进度与金币、分数及游戏记录仅保存在你的设备上，用于游戏运行及记住偏好，不会上传给我们。振动功能仅用于可选的游戏反馈。

保留和删除

本地数据保留在应用存储空间中，直到你在 Android 设置中清除应用存储或卸载游戏。清除存储会永久重置本地进度和偏好。本游戏没有需要注销的在线账号或服务器端游戏档案。

客服邮件

你主动发邮件时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和邮件内容，仅用于答复和解决请求；访问限于客服处理所需人员，保留期限限于处理请求或适用法律要求所需。你可通过同一邮箱请求删除。请勿发送密码、支付资料或不必要的敏感信息。邮件联系与游戏运行相互独立。

分享及外部服务

我们不出售游戏数据，也不向广告商分享游戏数据。Google Play、操作系统、邮箱服务及你主动打开的网站受其各自隐私政策约束。浏览本政策网页时，托管服务商可能处理 IP 地址等常规技术请求信息；这与离线游戏的数据处理不同。

目标年龄

本游戏面向 13 岁及以上玩家，不专门面向 13 岁以下儿童，不要求提供年龄或其他个人资料。

政策更新

数据处理方式发生变化时，我们会更新本政策和生效日期。本政策描述当前版本；新增功能引入前会同步更新相关说明。
